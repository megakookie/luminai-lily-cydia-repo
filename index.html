Snag

Download videos and audio with yt-dlp and play them on your iPhone. For jailbroken iOS 6 and later.

Installing Snag also installs the "Snag yt-dlp runtime" package (Python 3.12, yt-dlp, QuickJS), which runs yt-dlp on the phone itself.
